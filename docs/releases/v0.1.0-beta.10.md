# FCS-Scribe v0.1.0-beta.10

Experimental beta addressing **S07: settings changes and short sessions
indefinitely postponing journal compilation**.

## Changes since v0.1.0-beta.9

- Make compilation due once the **oldest durable uncompiled recording is
  30 minutes old**, including time closed or manual. Resume restores age from
  existing validated timestamps instead of starting another 30-minute wait.
- Preserve compilation through settings saves/reloads, folder changes,
  interval/manual transitions and UI deferral. Unchanged intervals also keep
  the recording deadline; changed intervals re-arm only recording.
- Show a persistent, wrapping **Pending compilation** notice with count,
  oldest UTC, due/overdue state and manual/recovery/error holds, separate from
  transient operation status.
- Never auto-compile in manual or settings-recovery mode. Returning to automatic
  mode makes overdue debt eligible at the next normal visible, unblocked tick,
  not as a settings-save or Resume side effect.
- Retain debt through hidden/minimized/busy windows, open menus, modal dialogs
  and failed compilation. Successful compilation clears pending work without
  shifting review; the next first pending recording starts a fresh age window.
- Do not schedule empty periodic compilations. Keep original sessions,
  idempotent journal writes, current-state/draft checks and no-silent-mod-save
  behavior.
- Derive age in memory without changing tracking/journal/packet/workspace
  schemas or rewriting stored timestamps. Update the wiki and plan;
  S08-S16 remain open.

## Installation and compatibility

Requirements: Windows, .NET Framework 4.8, installed FCS Extended and the
inspected Steam FCS 2.14.2 executable with SHA-256:

```text
3688c4d8421d51c2d30c2ff0338874779d91eff150c6a02bf415bc1cdd9c7796
```

Close FCS, extract the archive and copy its `FCS-Scribe` folder into
`<Kenshi>\mods\FCS-Scribe`, replacing older plugin files when upgrading.
Launch FCS Extended and enable FCS-Scribe. Back up your mod projects and FCS
notes files first. Building/downloading does not install or change your game.
Existing tracking projects and valid settings need no migration; keep each
baseline and its `.scribe` folder together.

## Scheduling boundaries

Older pending recordings can become immediately eligible when you resume or
select automatic mode, but remain durable session data until compilation
succeeds. Manual mode requires **Compile journal**; settings recovery and
paused errors hold automation. Faulted journals still need reopen/validation.

UTC age includes closed/manual time; an already-running countdown uses the
monotonic window clock. A future timestamp encountered on Resume/new debt is
treated as due now, not a potentially unbounded delay. These are local
best-effort rules, not authenticated evidence or a trusted cross-restart clock.
Tracking is not a mod backup, and compilation never saves the mod or applies
notes. Large-model/history performance (S12) and full interactive FCS Extended
acceptance (S09) remain open.

## Validation and source

Recorded checks: **518 core**, **266 existing vanilla plus 16 settings recovery
and 24 scheduling**, and **265 existing representative-mod plus 16 settings
recovery and 24 scheduling** checks passed. The representative mod has no instances;
that mutation was explicitly skipped there and covered by vanilla.

The new matrix covers exact/sub-millisecond boundaries, repeated real settings
saves/reloads, folder/interval/manual transitions, aged short-session resumes,
manual/recovery holds, hidden/minimized/busy/menu/modal deferral, failed
compilation, paused-error wording, wrapping notice layout and independent
recording deadlines. Isolated 32-bit hosts keep scenario allocations separate;
they are not a production memory fix. Older timestamps/hashes are synthesized
only in disposable fixtures, not a claim of a full 30-minute interactive wait.

A separate check generated history with the released beta 9 DLL and verified
beta 10 replay/export with exact original evidence, state, pending timestamp
and unchanged source-file hashes. Exhaustive filesystem failures, power loss
and the complete real FCS Extended interaction remain unverified.

The ZIP contains the plugin, manifest, README, documentation, GPL-3.0 license
and `release.json` identifying the exact source commit. No Kenshi, FCS or
upstream FCS Extended binaries are bundled. Verify the ZIP against its
`.sha256` asset; the matching Git tag/source archives supply corresponding source.

# FCS-Scribe v0.1.0-beta.5

Experimental beta addressing **S02: AI handoff omitting accumulated recordings**.
The user-selected workflow chooses an inclusive recording range before export.

## Changes since v0.1.0-beta.4

- Add first/last recording selection with timestamps, sessions, source kinds,
  counts and an evidence/warning preview. Earlier sessions and both compiled
  and uncompiled entries remain selectable after resume.
- Label current unrecorded edits explicitly. Including them does not create a
  recording; exporting never advances tracking, compiles history or saves FCS.
- Retain context-only updates without allowing an all-context range to stand
  in for record-change documentation. Earlier gameplay evidence stays available
  after Workshop/context-only changes and unchanged reviews add no duplicates.
- Introduce schema-3 range handoffs containing original schema-1/2 packets and
  uniquely qualified `EntryId:original-change-ID` citations in the outer
  catalogue/template. Original batch/evidence identities and tracking files
  remain unchanged. Preserve observed reversals, not only net changes.
- Keep current-state stale-draft validation, even for historical ranges.
  Deleted historical targets require choosing a current survivor. S01's
  exactly-once evidence coverage and matching-completed-note retry still apply.
- Validate sources/build ranges on detached data in the background. Update
  the Copilot plan and wiki; S03-S16 remain open.

## Installation and compatibility

Requirements: Windows, .NET Framework 4.8, installed FCS Extended and the
inspected Steam FCS 2.14.2 executable with SHA-256:

```text
3688c4d8421d51c2d30c2ff0338874779d91eff150c6a02bf415bc1cdd9c7796
```

Close FCS, extract the archive and copy its `FCS-Scribe` folder into
`<Kenshi>\mods\FCS-Scribe`, replacing older plugin files when upgrading.
Launch FCS Extended and enable FCS-Scribe. Back up your mod projects and FCS
notes files first. Building/downloading does not install or change your game.

Tracking/session schemas, original schema-1/2 serialization and note formatting
are unchanged. Resume the original tracking JSON with its `.scribe` folder.
Schema-3 range packets are AI handoffs, not resumable baselines or conversion
inputs. Use only their **outer ResponseTemplate and qualified change IDs**;
the nested original templates are archival.

## Remaining boundaries

- Ranges are recorded observations, not a full event stream or a net diff.
  Overlapping selections can document the same evidence under different batch
  markers; exporting does not mark entries already documented.
- Original packets and qualified catalogues both ship in the export. Large
  selections increase size/memory use; preview and live capture remain on the
  UI thread. S12 profiling remains open.
- Current captured state must still match export before notes apply. Draft
  persistence, transactional notes/rollback, Workshop capture coupling and
  other S03-S16 work remain unresolved.
- Tracking is not a mod backup. Never share full tracking JSONs/backups or
  their private loaded-record database with AI or Workshop; inspect compact
  handoffs for private content before sharing.
- Full interactive FCS Extended acceptance and broader stacks/builds remain
  unverified. Disposable form/method checks do not close that gate.

## Validation and source

Recorded checks: **202 core**, **153 disposable vanilla FCS** and **152
representative-mod FCS** checks passed. The representative mod has no instances;
that mutation was explicitly skipped there and covered by vanilla.
Coverage includes several ticks/sessions, restart/compilation/archived sessions,
preserved original identities, qualified evidence integrity, context-only and
pending entries, no export-time history mutation, selector bounds/preview/
cancellation and stale-state rejection. Actual FCS notes checks use range
evidence through matching-completed-note retry and normal save/reload.
A separate check generated real beta 4 compiled/uncompiled history with the
released binary and verified that beta 5 replays/exports it while preserving
exact original packet serialization and every tracking source-file hash.

The ZIP contains the plugin, manifest, README, documentation, GPL-3.0 license
and `release.json` identifying the exact source commit. No Kenshi, FCS or
upstream FCS Extended binaries are bundled. Verify the ZIP against its
`.sha256` asset; the matching Git tag/source archives supply corresponding source.
